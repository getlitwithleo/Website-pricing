    FAQ

    Common questions

    Do I need my text and photos ready?

    It helps, but it isn't required. Copywriting is available for $10 a page. Your turnaround begins once all of your content has been received.

    What counts as a revision round?

    One round is one list of changes sent together. Send everything you'd like changed in a single message and it will all be handled in that round.

    Is hosting included?

    Setup is included with Small Business and above, and is a $20 add-on for smaller jobs. The hosting plan and domain name are paid directly to the provider.

    Can I upgrade later?

    Yes. You can move to a larger package at any time and pay only the difference between the two.

    How does payment work?

    50% up front to reserve your spot, and the remaining 50% when your site goes live.

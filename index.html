    Questions

    Good questions

    Do I need my text and photos ready?

    It helps, but no stress if you don't. Add copywriting for $10 a page and I'll write it for you. Your turnaround starts once I have everything I need.

    What counts as a revision round?

    One round is one list of changes sent together. Put everything you want changed in a single message and I'll make all of it in one go.

    Is hosting included?

    Setting it up is included from the Small Business level up, or $20 on the smaller levels. The hosting plan and domain name are paid to the provider directly.

    Can I upgrade to a bigger level later?

    Yes. Start small and move up whenever you're ready. You just pay the difference between the two levels.

    How does payment work?

    Half up front to book your spot, and the other half when your site goes live.

    The fine print

    • Prices are in US dollars and cover the work listed for each level.
    • Turnaround starts once your text, photos and logo arrive.
    • Domain names, hosting plans, paid plugins and stock photos are billed at cost.
    • Anything outside the listed work gets quoted before I start.
    • Quotes are good for 30 days.

    Got a project in mind?

    Tell me what you're working on and I'll tell you which level fits. Asking is always free.

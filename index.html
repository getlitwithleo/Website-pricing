    FAQ

    Common questions

    Can I mix features from different packages?

    Yes. Pick the package closest to what you need and add the rest from the add-ons, like a booking calendar on a Portfolio. Anything not listed gets quoted before work starts.

    Do I need my text and photos ready?

    It helps, but it isn't required. Copywriting is available for $10 a page. Your turnaround begins once all of your content has been received.

    What counts as a revision round?

    One round is one list of changes sent together. Send everything you'd like changed in a single message and it will all be handled in that round.

    Is hosting included?

    Getting your site live is included. You buy the hosting plan and domain name directly from the provider in your name, and you get simple steps for connecting your domain.

    Can I upgrade later?

    Yes. You can move to a bigger package at any time and pay only the difference between the two.

    How does payment work?

    50% up front to reserve your spot, and the remaining 50% when your site goes live.

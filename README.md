# Website Design Pricing

A one-page pricing page for website design work, from a $20 tune-up to a $400 fully custom masterpiece. The three main packages ($150, $250, $400) lead the page, with the $250 Pro marked as recommended; the $20 and $75 jobs sit below as smaller jobs.

Open `index.html` in a browser to view it. It's a single self-contained file, so you can host it anywhere (for example, GitHub Pages).

## Levels

| Level | Name | Price | Pages | Turnaround | Revision rounds |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Tune-Up | $20 | Your existing site | 2 days | 1 |
| 2 | One-Pager | $75 | 1 | 5 days | 1 |
| 3 | Small Business | $150 | Up to 5 | 10 days | 2 |
| 4 | Pro (recommended) | $250 | Up to 8 | 14 days | 3 |
| 5 | Masterpiece | $400 | Up to 12 | 21 days | 5 |

## What's on the page

- A hero with a desktop and phone mockup, and a strip of what every website includes
- Package cards with pages, turnaround, revisions and features; each "Choose" button preselects that package in the quote builder
- A side-by-side comparison table
- Add-ons, a quote builder (total, 50% deposit, balance due at launch), the process, FAQ, and terms

## Editing prices

Prices appear in the hero, the package cards, the comparison table, the add-ons list and the quote builder. If you change one, update each spot, plus the `LEVELS` object and add-on amounts in the `<script>` at the bottom of `index.html`.

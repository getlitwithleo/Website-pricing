How to brew it

Gentle water, long steep

White tea is delicate. Let the water cool for a few minutes, then give the buds time to open.

Water

Let a boiled kettle rest for four minutes.

Steep

Three minutes, then add a minute for each refill.

Leaf

About two heaped teaspoons of buds per cup.

3 cups

The same buds brew three times, each one sweeter.

Scenting
Nine nights with jasmine

Only the unopened buds are picked, on a few clear days each spring. They're covered in fine silver hair, which is where the name comes from.

In summer, when the jasmine blooms, the buds are piled with fresh flowers overnight. In the morning the spent flowers are sifted out and new ones added. Our tea is scented nine times.

The result smells strongly of jasmine but tastes soft and honeyed, never perfumey.

First-flush Darjeeling

The first picking of spring from a Himalayan garden: bright and brisk, with the grape-like muscatel note Darjeeling is famous for.

How to brew it

Hot, but not boiling

First flush is lighter than most black teas. Slightly cooler water keeps it bright instead of bitter. Best without milk.

Water

Let a boiled kettle rest for one minute.

Steep

Three minutes. Taste at two if you like it lighter.

Leaf

One heaped teaspoon per cup.

2 cups

A second, longer steep is still lovely.

First flush is the first growth of the year, picked for about ten days in March after the bushes wake up from winter.

The cold nights at 2,000 meters slow the leaves down, which concentrates their flavor. That's where the bright, almost grape-like muscatel note comes from.

Mei spent a week in the garden at first flush. You can read about it in the journal.

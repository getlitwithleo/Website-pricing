Phoenix Oolong

A single-bush oolong from Phoenix Mountain, with an orchid fragrance, a taste of ripe apricot and a warm, toasty finish.

  • Orchid
  • Stone fruit
  • Toasty
  • Long finish

How to brew it

Hot water, many cups

Oolong loves water just off the boil. Brew it in short steeps and keep going: the flavor changes with every cup.

Water

Just off the boil.

Steep

Two minutes, or 30 seconds in a small pot.

Leaf

A good pinch of twisted leaves per cup.

6 cups

The leaves give six cups or more.

Where it's from

Phoenix Mountain

Garden
Wudong village, single bush
Region
Chaozhou, Guangdong, China
Altitude
1,100 m
Harvest
Spring, April 2026
Roast
Light charcoal

Each Phoenix oolong comes from a named bush, some of them hundreds of years old. Ours is picked from one family's trees in Wudong village, near the top of the mountain.

The leaves are bruised by hand, partly oxidized and then lightly roasted over charcoal, which gives the toasty finish.

It's the tea we pour most often at Saturday tastings, and the one guests most often take home.

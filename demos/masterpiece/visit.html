Get in touch

Private events, wholesale and questions

We reply within a day. For tasting bookings, use the tastings page.

Can we book the whole room?

Yes, on Friday evenings, for up to eight guests. See private tastings, or write to us for larger groups.

Do you supply restaurants?

We supply a handful of restaurants and cafés with loose-leaf tea and staff training. Ask for our wholesale list.

Do you sell gift cards?

In any amount, in the tea room or by email, printed on a letterpress card.

What we believe

Three rules, never broken

Single estate

Every tea comes from one garden, so you taste one place and one season.

Picked by hand

Only the top two leaves and a bud, picked by people who've done it for decades.

Brewed to order

Weighed, timed and poured at the right temperature, every single cup.

A good cup of tea asks you to sit down for five minutes. We think that's the whole point.

Our story

Halcyon started with one kettle and a folding table at a weekend market, pouring free tastings of a single green tea.

Today the tea house serves forty teas from eleven gardens, but the idea hasn't changed: find the best leaves, then get out of their way.

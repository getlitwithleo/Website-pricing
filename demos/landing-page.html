Schedule

How the night goes

  1. 5:00 pm
    Gates open

    Stalls open and the first band starts by the river.

  2. 6:30 pm
    Lantern lighting

    All 400 lanterns light up at once. Find a spot on the bridge.

  3. 7:00 pm
    Headline set

    Live music on the main lawn until 9.

  4. 9:30 pm
    Fire dancers

    A 20-minute show over the water.

  5. 11:00 pm
    Last call

    Stalls close. Lanterns stay lit until midnight.

FAQ

Good to know

Is it really free?

Yes. Entry is free for everyone. Bring cash or a card for the stalls.

Where do I park?

The Riverside garage is $5 for the night, and the number 12 bus stops at the main gate.

Can I bring my dog?

Friendly dogs on a leash are welcome. Water bowls are by the info tent.

What if it rains?

Light rain, we're on. Heavy rain moves us to the following Saturday, and RSVPs get a text first.

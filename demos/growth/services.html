Questions

Before you sign up

Is there a contract?

No. Plans are month to month, and you can cancel with 30 days' notice. We'll hand over clean books either way.

What software do you use?

We work in QuickBooks Online or Xero. If you don't have either, we'll set one up and the subscription is billed to you directly.

I'm behind by a few months. Can you help?

Yes. Catch-up work is quoted separately, usually $150 to $300 per month of backlog, and done before your first monthly report.

Do you file my taxes?

We get your books tax-ready and work directly with your tax preparer. If you don't have one, we'll recommend someone we trust.

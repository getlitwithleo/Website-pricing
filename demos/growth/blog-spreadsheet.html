Signs you've outgrown your spreadsheet

Spreadsheets are a great way to start keeping books. But a few signs mean it's time to move on, before the spreadsheet starts costing you money.

Rachel Kim · Founder, ClearpathSep 30, 2026 · 3 min read

Almost every business starts its books in a spreadsheet. It's free, it's flexible, and for the first year or two it's all you need. Then, slowly, it isn't.

Four signs it's time

  • It takes more than an hour a week. If updating it has become a part-time job, software will give you that time back.
  • You've found the same transaction twice. Copy and paste errors are the most common spreadsheet mistake, and the hardest to spot.
  • You can't easily tell who owes you money. If chasing invoices means scrolling and guessing, you're probably missing some.
  • You dread opening it. Honestly, this is the most reliable sign of all.

What to switch to

Bookkeeping software like QuickBooks Online or Xero connects straight to your bank and card accounts, pulls in every transaction, and sorts most of them automatically once it learns your patterns. Invoices, bills and reports live in one place.

Switching mid-year? It's easier than it sounds. Your bookkeeper can bring this year's numbers across and keep last year's spreadsheet as a record.

Paired with a monthly check from a bookkeeper, it takes the whole job off your plate, and your spreadsheet can go back to planning the office party.

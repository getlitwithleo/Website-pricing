Schedule

How the night goes

  1. 5:00 pm
    Gates open

    Stalls open and the first band starts by the river.

  2. 6:30 pm
    Lights on

    Every string light in the park switches on at once. Find a spot on the bridge.

  3. 7:00 pm
    Headline set

    Live music on the main lawn until 9.

  4. 9:30 pm
    Fire dancers

    A 20-minute show over the water.

  5. 11:00 pm
    Last call

    Stalls close. The lights stay on until midnight.

Getting there

Find your way to the lights

  • Bus

    The number 12 stops right at the main gate, every 10 minutes until midnight.

  • Car

    The Riverside garage on River Road is $5 for the night. It fills up by 7.

  • Bike

    Free valet bike parking by the main gate, staffed until close.

  • Access

    Paved paths throughout, accessible restrooms by the food hall, and a quiet tent by the info desk.

FAQ

Good to know

Is it really free?

Yes. Entry is free for everyone. Bring cash or a card for the stalls.

Where do I park?

The Riverside garage is $5 for the night, and the number 12 bus stops at the main gate.

Can I bring my dog?

Friendly dogs on a leash are welcome. Water bowls are by the info tent.

What if it rains?

Light rain, we're on. Heavy rain moves us to the following Saturday, and RSVPs get a text first.

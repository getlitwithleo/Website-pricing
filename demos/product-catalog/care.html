Shipping and care

Orders, shipping and looking after your pottery

Everything about how ordering works, plus how to keep handmade pieces looking good for years.

How ordering works

  • Add pieces to your order list, or tap "Order now" on any product.
  • Send the list as a text or email. It's written for you.
  • We reply within a day to confirm stock and arrange pickup or shipping.
  • Pay at pickup, or by bank transfer before we ship.

Caring for your pottery

  • Mugs, plates and bowls are dishwasher and microwave safe.
  • Avoid sudden temperature changes, like a cold plate into a hot oven.
  • Unglazed bases can scratch tables. Use a coaster or felt pad.
  • Terracotta darkens with water. That's normal and it lightens again as it dries.

Returns and breakages

If something arrives broken, send us a photo within 7 days and we'll replace it free. Changed your mind? Return unused pieces within 14 days for a refund, minus shipping.

Small marks, speckles and glaze variations are part of handmade pottery, not faults.

Questions

Do you take custom orders?

Yes, for sets of 6 or more in a glaze from the shop. Message us with what you'd like and we'll quote a price and time.

Will my piece look exactly like the photo?

Very close, but every piece is made by hand, so speckles, glaze pooling and sizes vary a little.

Do you sell gift cards?

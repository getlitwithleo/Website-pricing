Shop / Owner's guide

Adding a product, step by step

A guide for the shop owner. Adding a new piece takes about five minutes and doesn't need any code knowledge, just careful copying.

Demo noteThis page is the written guide that comes with the Product Catalog package. On a real site it's sent to you as a document, not shown to customers.

In this guide 1. Take the photo 2. Upload it 3. Open the product list 4. Copy and change a product 5. Save and check Sales, price changes and sold out Every field explained

01Take the photo

  • Shoot in daylight, near a window, on a plain background. The shop looks best when every photo has a similar light.
  • Crop it square. The shop shows every photo as a square, so anything outside the square won't be seen.
  • Make it about 1,200 pixels wide. Bigger photos slow the site down; smaller ones look blurry on big screens.
  • Name the file in lowercase with dashes, starting with pc-. For example: pc-blue-jug.jpg.

Want two or three photos of one piece? Name them pc-blue-jug.jpg, pc-blue-jug-2.jpg and so on. The first one is the main photo.

02Upload it

  1. Go to your site's repository on GitHub and open the demos/img folder (on your live site, simply img).
  2. Click Add file, then Upload files, and drag your photo in.
  3. Click Commit changes. That's GitHub's word for save.

03Open the product list

Every product lives in one file called products.js. Open it on GitHub and click the pencil icon to edit it. You'll see a list of products, each one inside curly brackets { }.

04Copy and change a product

Find a product that's similar to your new one, copy its whole block from { to }, and paste it right below. Then change the details. Here's a new jug, based on the mug:

{ id: "blue-jug", name: "Blue Pouring Jug", cat: "Tableware", img: ["pc-blue-jug"], price: 42,
  sizes: [["Small", 36], ["Large", 42]], finishes: ["Sky blue", "Charcoal"], badge: "new", added: 26,
  desc: "A round-bellied jug for milk, water or flowers. Pours without dripping.",
  details: ["6 in tall", "Holds 24 oz", "Dishwasher safe"] },

The three things people miss: keep the quote marks around words, keep the comma at the very end of the block, and give added a number higher than every other product so it shows first under "Newest".

05Save and check

  1. Click Commit changes at the top right.
  2. Wait one or two minutes while the site updates.
  3. Open the shop, search for your new piece, and click it to check the photo, sizes and price.

If the shop shows no products at all, there's a typo in products.js, usually a missing comma or quote mark. Open the file's history on GitHub, compare with the last version, and fix the line you changed.

06Sales, price changes and sold out

  • Change a price: change the number after price: (and inside sizes, if the piece has sizes).
  • Put something on sale: add was: 32, with the old price, set price to the new one, and set badge: "sale".
  • Mark something new: add badge: "new",. Remove it after a month or two.
  • Sold out for good: delete the whole block from { to },.

07Every field explained

FieldWhat it does
idA short name for the page address, lowercase with dashes. Must be different for every product.
nameThe name shown on the card and the product page.
catThe category. Use an existing one, or type a new one and it appears as a new filter button.
imgPhoto file names without .jpg, in square brackets.
priceThe price in dollars, no dollar sign.
wasOptional. The old price, shown crossed out.
sizesOptional. Pairs of size and price.
finishesGlaze or color choices, in quote marks.
badgeOptional. "new" or "sale".
addedA number for the "Newest" sort. Higher is newer.
descOne or two sentences about the piece.
detailsShort facts, shown as a list: size, care, materials.

Stuck? Email your web designer with a screenshot of what you changed. It's usually a one-character fix.

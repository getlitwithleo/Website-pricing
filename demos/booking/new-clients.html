New clients

New here? Welcome.

Here's how a first visit works, how to prepare, and a short form that tells your stylist what you're hoping for before you sit down.

What to expect

  1. 1
    A quick chat first

    15 minutes to talk through what you want, with photos if you have them. Free, and it's part of your first booking.

  2. 2
    Wash, cut or color

    We explain each step and check in before anything big. Nothing gets cut or colored that we haven't agreed on.

  3. 3
    Style and aftercare

    You leave with tips and a product list for keeping it looking good at home, sent by text.

  4. 4
    How to prepare

    Come with dry hair, styled how you usually wear it, and bring photos of looks you love (and ones you don't).

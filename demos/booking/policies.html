Policies and FAQ

The small print, kept short

Our policies keep the calendar fair for everyone. Questions? Text us at (555) 014-9090.

Policies

Cancelling or moving

Free up to 24 hours before, using the link in your confirmation email. Later than that, there's a $20 fee. No-shows are charged half the service price.

Running late

Text us. We'll do everything we can, but after 15 minutes we may need to shorten your service or move your time.

Deposits

Appointments of 2 hours or more (balayage, highlights, all-over color) need a $30 deposit when you book. It comes off your bill.

Patch tests

New color clients need a quick patch test at least 48 hours before color. It's free: book a new client consult.

Kids

Kids' cuts are for under 12s. Please don't bring children along to your own appointment. Our chairs are small and scissors are sharp.

Not happy?

Tell us within 7 days and we'll fix it for free. We'd much rather know.

FAQ

Common questions

How do I book?

Online, any time, on the booking page. Pick a service, a stylist and a time, and you'll get a confirmation straight away.

Will I get a reminder?

Yes. A confirmation email when you book, and a reminder text the day before. Reply to the text to confirm or move it.

How much will color cost?

Our color prices are for shoulder-length hair. Long or thick hair may take longer, and we'll always quote you before we start.

Do you do curly hair?

Yes. Kai specializes in curly and coily hair, and cuts dry, curl by curl.

What payment do you take?

All major cards, tap-to-pay and cash. Tips can go on card.

Do you sell gift cards?

Yes, in any amount, at the front desk or by phone.

About

Theo pours, scrapes and layers acrylic and ink until a painting starts to look like the place it came from. Most pieces begin as a ten-minute sketch made outside at dusk and take a month to finish in the studio.

He takes a small number of commissions each year, usually for a specific room and a specific view.

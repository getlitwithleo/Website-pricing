About Theo

Theo pours, scrapes and layers acrylic and ink until a painting starts to look like the place it came from. Most pieces begin as a ten-minute sketch made outside at dusk and take a month to finish in the studio.

He grew up on the coast, studied printmaking, and spent eight years designing book covers before painting full time in 2022. The studio is a converted boat shed a few minutes from the estuary, which explains a lot about the paintings.

He takes a small number of commissions each year, usually for a specific room and a specific view.

Commissions

A commission starts with a room and a view: the place the painting will hang, and the place it's about. Theo takes four to six a year, and each one takes about two months.

  1. Tell me about the space

    Send photos of the room, the wall and the light, and a place that matters to you.

  2. Sketches

    Three small studies on paper within two weeks. You pick one, or we try again.

  3. Painting

    About six weeks in the studio, with photos as it goes. A 50% deposit starts the work.

  4. Delivery

    Varnished, wired and ready to hang. Free delivery within 50 miles.

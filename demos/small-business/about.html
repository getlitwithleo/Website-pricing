Three mechanics, one neighborhood

We opened in a one-car garage on Brightside Avenue in 2012. We're still on the same street, just with more room and better coffee.

Our story

Fix it right, tell it straight

Sam Ortiz started Brightside after one too many shops told a friend she needed a new bike when all she needed was a $15 brake adjustment. The rule from day one: quote every job before we start, and never sell a repair nobody needs.

Twelve years later, we've fixed more than 9,000 bikes, from first balance bikes to e-cargo bikes hauling two kids and the groceries. Most of our customers walk in, and most of them we know by name.

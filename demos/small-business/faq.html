Repairs

Do I need an appointment?

No. Walk in any time we're open. For an overhaul or a bike fit, call ahead so we can save you a spot.

How long will my repair take?

Flats take about 20 minutes. Most tune-ups and adjustments are ready the same day if you drop off before 10 am. Overhauls take 2 to 3 days.

Can you pick up my bike?

Yes. Pickup and drop-off is $20 within 3 miles of the shop. Book it by phone or with the contact form.

Do you work on any bike?

Road, mountain, hybrid, kids', cargo and most e-bikes. We don't work on gas-powered bikes or scooters.

Prices and payment

How do I know what it'll cost?

We quote every job before we start, and we call you if anything changes. All our labor prices are on the services page.

Do parts cost extra?

Yes. Parts are priced separately, and we always tell you the price first. You're welcome to bring your own parts.

What payment do you take?

Cash, all major cards, and tap-to-pay. Payment is due at pickup.

Is there a warranty on repairs?

Every repair has a 90-day guarantee. If something we fixed slips or squeaks, bring it back and we'll fix it free.

E-bikes

Do you work on e-bikes?

Yes, on most major brands. We test the battery and motor before quoting any repair.

Can you replace my e-bike battery?

Usually. We'll test the old one first, since a loose connector is often the real problem. If it does need replacing, we order the right one for your bike.

Used bikes

Do you sell bikes?

A small selection of used bikes, all fully overhauled, with a 3-month warranty. See what's in stock on the services page.

Will you buy my old bike?

Sometimes. Send us a photo and the brand with the contact form and we'll make you an offer, or take it as credit toward a repair.

Still have a question?

Call or text the shop. We answer during opening hours, usually within the hour.

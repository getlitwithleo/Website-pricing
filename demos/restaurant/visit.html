Our story

A fig tree, a lease and a lot of butter

Ines and Tomás Navarro opened Little Fig in 2017, named after the fig tree that still grows in the courtyard out back. Ines bakes. Tomás makes the coffee and knows everyone's order.

Everything in the case is made here, starting at 5 am. Our coffee comes from Pine Street Roasters two streets away, our eggs from a farm 40 minutes north, and the figs, in late summer, from that tree.

Questions

Before you come

Do I need a reservation?

Not on weekdays. For weekend brunch, or groups of five or more, we recommend booking a table.

Can I work from the café on my laptop?

Of course, on weekdays. There's free Wi-Fi and outlets along the bench. On weekends we keep the tables laptop-free so everyone gets a seat.

Do you have gluten-free or vegan options?

Plenty. Use the filters on the menu page to see them. Our kitchen does handle wheat and nuts, so please tell us about any allergy.

Do you sell gift cards?

Yes, in any amount, at the counter or by phone.

Can I bring a cake for a birthday?

Yes, there's no cake fee. Let us know when you book and we'll bring plates and candles.
